This notice explains how DAVAN Technologies handles information submitted through its careers application forms.

INFORMATION COLLECTED

What applicants submit.

We collect the details you provide in the application form: name, email address, education, city, current work status, current workplace (when applicable), selected internship, resume, skills and optional achievements or project details.

WHY WE USE IT

Recruitment and selection.

Application information is used to review candidates, assess relevant experience, contact applicants and coordinate recruitment for the selected role. Resume text is processed in your browser to suggest skills; those suggestions are editable before you submit.

WHERE IT IS STORED

Private application storage.

Application records are stored in a DAVAN Technologies Supabase project in the Mumbai region. Resume PDFs are stored in a private storage bucket and are not publicly listed. Access to applicant information is restricted to authorised recruitment reviewers.

RETENTION & REQUESTS

Ask about your information.

Application details are retained only as long as needed for recruitment and related records. To ask about, correct or request deletion of your application information, contact hello.gensolutions@gmail.com from the email address used in your application.

IMPORTANT

Share only what is relevant.

Do not upload identity documents, financial records, passwords, confidential employer information or other sensitive details unrelated to the role. The contact form on this site opens an email draft and does not store submissions on this website.

This notice should be reviewed and updated if the recruitment process, data processors or retention practices change.
